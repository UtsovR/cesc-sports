Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project: CESC Officers Sports Club Brand Messaging Refresh

Prepared for: CESC Officers Sports Club
Prepared by: Project Owner
Version: 2.0
Date: April 2026

1. Project Overview

This PRD defines a brand-led content refresh for the CESC Officers Sports Club website centered around the statement:

"Building champions and fostering sporting excellence since 1995."

The purpose of this update is to strengthen the club's identity, highlight its legacy, and present a consistent message across key website sections. The new messaging should communicate heritage, credibility, community impact, and the club's long-term contribution to sporting excellence.

2. Goals & Objectives
Primary Goals

Establish a clear and memorable brand statement for the club

Highlight the club's legacy since 1995

Improve the emotional and institutional value of the website content

Create stronger alignment between the club's history, mission, and public presentation

Secondary Goals

Improve homepage storytelling

Strengthen user trust and brand recall

Create consistent language across major sections of the website

3. Core Brand Statement

Primary Statement

Building champions and fostering sporting excellence since 1995.

Brand Meaning

Building champions refers to nurturing talent, discipline, teamwork, and achievement across sports.

Fostering sporting excellence reflects the club's long-term commitment to high standards, active participation, and continuous growth.

Since 1995 reinforces legacy, trust, and the club's established presence.

4. Functional Requirements
4.1 Homepage Hero Messaging
Current Need

The website needs a stronger opening message that immediately communicates the club's identity and legacy.

Required Change

Add the new brand statement prominently in the homepage hero section.

Functional Behavior

The homepage must display:

Club name

Primary brand statement

Supporting copy that reinforces legacy, sportsmanship, and community participation

Suggested Content Direction

Headline:
Building champions and fostering sporting excellence since 1995.

Supporting line example:
CESC Officers Sports Club has been empowering employees and families through competitive spirit, teamwork, and a culture of excellence for decades.

UI Requirement

The statement must be clearly readable on desktop and mobile.

It should appear above the fold.

It should feel like the primary identity message of the page.

4.2 About Section Content Refresh
Current Need

The About section should better communicate the club's story and long-standing contribution.

Required Change

Update the About section content to align with the new brand statement.

Functional Behavior

The About section should explain:

When the club was established

Its role in promoting sports culture

Its contribution to building champions and encouraging participation

Its community and institutional value

Content Direction

The About section should include a short heritage narrative mentioning 1995 and the club's continued commitment to developing sporting talent and character.

4.3 Vision & Mission Alignment
Current Need

The Vision & Mission section should reflect the new positioning language.

Required Change

Revise or align the Vision & Mission copy so it supports the new statement.

Functional Behavior

Vision should communicate long-term sporting growth, participation, and excellence.

Mission should communicate development, opportunity, discipline, inclusion, and competitive spirit.

Content Expectation

The wording should connect naturally with:

champions

sporting excellence

legacy

community development

4.4 Legacy and Trust Positioning
Current Need

The website should visibly communicate that the club has a long and credible history.

Required Change

Introduce heritage-focused messaging elements across appropriate sections.

Functional Behavior

The site should reference the club's foundation year where relevant.

Possible placement areas:

Homepage hero

About section

Footer

Section introductions

Content Requirement

The year 1995 should be presented as a credibility marker, not just a date.

Example phrases:

Serving the sporting community since 1995

A legacy of sporting excellence since 1995

4.5 Content Consistency Across Pages
Current Need

Website messaging should feel unified rather than fragmented.

Required Change

Use the new brand statement and its supporting tone consistently across major public-facing pages.

Affected Areas

Home

About

Vision & Mission

Gallery introduction text

Registration or participation invitation text

Footer tagline or summary line

Functional Behavior

All revised copy should maintain a consistent tone:

inspiring

trustworthy

institutional

community-oriented

4.6 Optional Supporting Content Block
Recommended Enhancement

Add a short supporting section or highlight block that reinforces the brand message.

Possible Section Themes

Our Legacy

Our Sporting Journey

Three Decades of Excellence

Functional Behavior

This section may include:

A short paragraph about the club's journey since 1995

A few values such as discipline, teamwork, excellence, and participation

A simple call to action encouraging users to explore events, register, or engage with the club

5. Non-Functional Requirements
Clarity

The message must be simple, clear, and easy to understand.

Consistency

The same brand tone must be maintained across sections.

Responsiveness

All updated content must remain visually balanced on desktop and mobile.

Accessibility

Text placement and contrast must remain readable and accessible.

Brand Fit

The messaging must feel formal, motivating, and aligned with a respected sports institution.

6. Content Guidelines

The writing should be:

Warm

Motivational

Professional

Heritage-focused

Community-driven

Avoid:

Overly casual language

Generic sports cliches without institutional context

Inconsistent tone between sections

7. Success Criteria

The update will be considered successful when:

The new brand statement is visible in the homepage hero

The About section clearly references the club's legacy since 1995

The Vision & Mission section aligns with the new positioning

Site-wide messaging feels more consistent and purposeful

The website communicates both heritage and sporting ambition clearly

8. Acceptance Criteria

Feature will be considered complete when:

The statement "Building champions and fostering sporting excellence since 1995." is incorporated into the website content plan

Homepage messaging is updated to reflect the new statement

About content includes a legacy-driven narrative

Vision & Mission content supports the same brand direction

Supporting text across relevant sections follows a unified tone

The updated messaging is readable and effective on both desktop and mobile

9. Priority Level
High Priority

Homepage hero messaging update

About section content refresh

Vision & Mission alignment

Medium Priority

Legacy positioning in supporting sections

Footer or site-wide supporting tagline usage

Low Priority

Optional legacy highlight block

Additional copy refinements in secondary pages

10. Summary

This PRD introduces a brand and messaging refresh built around the statement:

"Building champions and fostering sporting excellence since 1995."

The goal is to ensure that the website presents CESC Officers Sports Club as a legacy-driven, excellence-focused institution with a strong sporting identity, a clear voice, and a unified message across all major sections.
